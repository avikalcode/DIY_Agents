REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY: ELECTRONICS & GROCERY

===============================================================================
1. ELECTRONICS CATEGORY
===============================================================================

1.1 Cancellation Rules
  * Pre-Delivery Cancellation: Full refund is applicable if the cancellation request 
    is submitted and processed before the item leaves the fulfillment center or is 
    dispatched for delivery.
  * Post-Dispatch Cancellation: If cancelled while in transit, a restocking fee 
    may apply, unless the delivery timeline constraint (Section 1.3) is breached.

1.2 Damage, Defects, and Discrepancies
  * Wrong or Damaged Items: Must be reported within 10 hours of the recorded delivery 
    timestamp. Photographic or video evidence of the damaged item and packaging is 
    strictly required for verification.
  * Malfunctioning or Dead on Arrival (DOA): If the item is not working or stops 
    working under normal usage parameters, it must be reported within 48 hours of 
    delivery. 
  * Missing Accessories / Components: Must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

1.3 Logistics and Delivery Delay Constraints
  * Delayed Delivery Breach: A full refund option is triggered if the item is not 
    delivered within 24 hours of the original committed delivery date/time slot, 
    excluding delays caused entirely by customer unavailability or incorrect address 
    provisions.

1.4 Return Constraints & Prerequisites
  * Serial Number Verification: The serial number/IMEI of the returned electronic item 
    must perfectly match the recorded serial number shipped from the warehouse.
  * Original Packaging: Items must be returned in their original box with all product 
    manuals, cables, accessories, and warranty cards intact.
  * Data Erasure: Users must wipe personal data, sign out of cloud accounts, and remove 
    passwords before returning smart devices. Factory-locked items will void the refund.

===============================================================================
2. GROCERY CATEGORY
===============================================================================

2.1 Cancellation Rules
  * Strict Window: Cancellations are only permitted within 30 minutes after the order 
    request has been raised and accepted by the merchant/fulfillment center.
  * Post-30 Min Lockdown: Once procurement or packaging begins after the 30-minute 
    window, the order cannot be cancelled, and no refund will be issued for change of mind.

2.2 Quality, Freshness, and Discrepancies
  * Quality Issues (Wrong/Not Fresh/Rotten/Expired): Must be reported within 1 hour 
    of the recorded delivery timestamp. 
  * Evidence Requirements: High-resolution clear photographs showing the spoilage, 
    rot, or the incorrect item label must be uploaded at the time of raising the complaint.
  * Missing Items: Unreceived items marked as delivered must be reported within 2 hours 
    of the delivery timestamp for immediate wallet credit or re-delivery.

2.3 Logistics and Delivery Delay Constraints
  * Delayed Delivery Breach: A full refund option is triggered if the grocery order is 
    not delivered within 1 hour of the original committed delivery time slot.

2.4 Return Constraints & Prerequisites
  * Hygiene and Perishability: Rotten or compromised perishable goods do not require 
    physical return to the delivery agent if photo evidence is approved; customers are 
    advised to safely discard them.
  * Non-Perishable Groceries: Packaged items (e.g., canned foods, dry goods) must have 
    their safety seals fully unbroken, packaging unsoiled, and labels intact to qualify.

===============================================================================
3. GENERAL PROCESSING TERMS (APPLICABLE TO BOTH CATEGORIES)
===============================================================================
  * Mode of Refund: Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment source 
    within 5-7 business days, or instantly credited to the user's platform wallet.
  * Abuse Policy: Multiple sequential refund claims flagged for missing items or fake 
    damages will trigger an automated fraud review and potential account suspension.
